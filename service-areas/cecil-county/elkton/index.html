Care in Elkton

How PHG fits this community

Being the hospital town cuts both ways: Elkton residents are close to emergency and inpatient care, but coming home from a stay at ChristianaCare Union Hospital is where support often thins out. A post-discharge RN visit—vital signs, medication reconciliation against the discharge paperwork, and clear red-flag education—covers the riskiest days at home.

Elkton is also where much of the county’s rental housing, senior housing, and multi-generational households concentrate. For adults managing without nearby family, RN-supervised personal care and scheduled aide check-ins keep daily life steady without moving anyone out of their home.
