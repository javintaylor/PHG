What determines coverage for an address.

A county name starts the conversation. The details of the request determine whether PHG can safely accept and schedule the visit.

Client Address
The exact home or community location establishes travel distance and confirms that the setting is within PHG’s current mobile reach.
Requested Service
The type of skilled nursing or aide support determines the clinical review, personnel, equipment, and time required.
Staff Availability
Coverage depends on an appropriately qualified PHG professional being available for the requested date and location.
Clinical and Setting Fit
An RN reviews whether the requested care and environment can be served safely within PHG’s scope.

From address to confirmed visit.

The coverage review is designed to give clients and families a clear answer before plans are made.

  1. Share the address and need

    Provide the service location, requested care, preferred timing, and the best contact for follow-up.

  2. Confirm geographic coverage

    PHG reviews the address against the current mobile service area and travel requirements.

  3. Complete RN review

    A registered nurse evaluates clinical fit and identifies any provider authorization or additional information needed.

  4. Confirm the visit

    When accepted, PHG coordinates the appropriate clinician or aide and confirms the appointment details.
