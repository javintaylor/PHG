Care shaped to the actual need.

Every request receives RN review. Provider authorization is obtained when medications or selected procedures require it.

Skilled nursing

Diagnosis-driven, visit-specific clinical support delivered under RN scope and documented oversight.

  • Comprehensive RN assessment and episodic plan of care
  • Provider-authorized IV therapy and infusion
  • Medication administration and injections
  • Wound, ostomy, and continence care
  • Urinary catheter changes and ostomy support
  • Non-emergent monitoring and observation

Personal assistance

Daily support provided by trained aides under RN delegation and supervision.

  • Bathing, dressing, grooming, and toileting
  • Mobility, transfers, and home safety support
  • Meal preparation and light housekeeping
  • Transportation accompaniment

Selected procedures

Appropriate diagnostics and procedures completed privately at home when clinically indicated.

  • Mobile phlebotomy and specimen handling
  • 12-lead EKG acquisition
  • Non-emergent nebulizer treatments
  • Ordered suture or staple removal

View all frequently asked questions
Is care covered by insurance?

PHG is private pay and does not submit claims to Medicare, Medicaid, or commercial insurance. Review PHG pricing and payment options, including potential HSA or FSA use for qualified medical expenses subject to your plan rules.

Do I need a provider order?

Not for every service. Medications, laboratory collection, and selected procedures require an appropriate order or real-time provider authorization. We identify required documentation during intake.

How quickly can a visit be arranged?

Timing depends on the service, clinical appropriateness, authorization requirements, staffing, and travel distance. Some appropriate referrals may be scheduled within 24 hours.

How is PHG different from traditional Home Health?

PHG is a private-pay Residential Service Agency. Patients do not need to be homebound or meet Medicare Home Health coverage criteria. Skilled services are episodic and visit specific.

Is PHG a replacement for 911 or my physician?

No. PHG serves clinically stable adults with non-emergent needs and does not replace emergency services, primary care, specialist care, or longitudinal medical management. Call 911 for a medical emergency.
