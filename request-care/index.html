A considered first step.

Provide the essentials for an initial nursing review. PHG will clarify the care need, required orders, pricing, and scheduling before any visit is confirmed.

Do not use this form for an emergency

Call 911 or seek emergency care now for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.

  • Chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, or signs of stroke
  • Uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness, or seizure
  • Severe abdominal pain with fever, rigidity, fainting, or persistent vomiting
Call 911
