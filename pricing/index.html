Two rates. No guessing.

Professional time is billed by service type. Before care begins, PHG reviews the requested schedule and identifies separate charges that may apply. Review the complete PHG service directory.

Aide Services

$50per hour

RN-supervised assistance designed around the client's assessed needs and agreed care plan.

  • Personal care and daily living support
  • Mobility, meal, and companionship support as appropriate
  • RN oversight within the service plan

Registered Nurse Services

$125per hour

Skilled, episodic nursing delivered at home after clinical review and, when required, an appropriate provider order.

  • Skilled assessment and ordered nursing interventions
  • Care coordination and clinically relevant documentation
  • Education for the client and involved family

What may be separate: medications, IV therapy and infusion, injections, supplies, laboratory specimen collection, pharmacy, and outside-provider fees are not included in the hourly rate. Any known separate PHG charge is listed in the written estimate before service.

Membership adds access, not ambiguity.

PHG Membership is an administrative and access program. Clinical services remain client-initiated, separately reviewed, and subject to availability and scope.

PHG Membership

Monthly pricing to be published

Tier pricing is still being finalized. PHG will publish the amount before enrollment and will not add an undisclosed membership fee.

Concierge Access

Administrative access benefits may include RN phone guidance, provider escalation, priority scheduling when available, care coordination, a member visit rate, and waiver of the standard nonmember after-hours fee. No in-home visit time is included.

Access Plus

Includes Concierge Access benefits plus eligibility for one client-initiated 30-minute episodic visit per month when clinically appropriate, along with enhanced member rates. Unused visits do not roll over. Supplies and medications remain separate.

Premier

Includes Access Plus benefits with eligibility for up to four client-initiated 30-minute episodic visits per month, highest scheduling priority when available, and a preferred member rate. Unused visits do not roll over. Supplies and medications remain separate.

Membership boundary: Membership does not create a plan of care, guarantee staffing or appointments, provide emergency response, or include unlisted medications, supplies, IV fluids, injections, laboratory, pharmacy, or outside-provider charges.

Pricing questions, answered.

For service-specific details or a written care estimate, speak with PHG before scheduling.

Are the $50 and $125 rates the current hourly rates?

Yes. Aide services are $50 per hour and RN services are $125 per hour. A written estimate identifies the planned hours and any known separate PHG charges before service.

Are supplies and medications included?

No. Medications, IV fluids, injections, clinical supplies, laboratory, pharmacy, and outside-provider charges are separate when applicable.

Can I use HSA or FSA funds?

PHG accepts HSA and FSA cards. Whether a service is a qualified medical expense depends on applicable tax rules, your account, and the plan administrator. PHG cannot determine eligibility for you.

Will long-term care insurance reimburse PHG care?

It may, depending on the policy. PHG remains private pay and can provide itemized documentation. The policyholder is responsible for confirming coverage and submitting the claim.

Can I use VA benefits now?

No. VA contracting is a planned future pathway, not a currently available payment option. Standard private-pay terms apply unless PHG formally announces verified participation.

Does membership pay for all visits?

No. Membership provides administrative and access benefits defined by tier. It does not guarantee availability or include every clinical service, medication, or supply. Tier pricing will be published before enrollment.
